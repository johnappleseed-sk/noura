# Business Model

## Positioning
Noura is a governed commerce platform plus marketplace layer.

It is not just:
- a storefront builder
- a pure marketplace shell
- a simple merchant SaaS admin

It combines:
- commerce operating system
- master catalog governance
- shared inventory model
- multi-merchant network participation

## Business Models Supported
- B2C retail commerce
- B2B account and contract commerce
- C2C marketplace participation later
- multi-vendor marketplace
- partner contract stores
- branch-store operations
- merchant-managed stores

## Entry Strategy
Start with a focused vertical and region.

Do **not** launch as a generic marketplace first.

Recommended early wedge characteristics:
- fragmented catalogs
- repeated purchases
- multi-store or partner-store behavior
- need for both B2B and B2C support

## Primary Customers
- merchants with fragmented catalog and store operations
- distributors / wholesalers with B2B buying workflows
- retail groups with branch or partner stores
- later: end customers and C2C sellers

## Revenue Model
Use a hybrid model:
- platform subscription
- transaction / take-rate revenue
- premium modules later
- payment and logistics margin later

## Strategic Differentiation
- one governed master catalog
- no duplicate product truth
- reusable catalog across merchants and stores
- super inventory linked to canonical SKUs
- enterprise governance from startup stage

## North-Star Business Outcomes
- merchant activation
- supply density
- catalog reuse rate
- order conversion
- repeat buyer behavior
- contribution margin

## Year 1 Bias
Prioritize:
- supply quality
- merchant onboarding
- catalog governance
- reliable operations

Do not prioritize:
- broad consumer expansion
- uncontrolled open marketplace onboarding
- feature sprawl without activation impact
